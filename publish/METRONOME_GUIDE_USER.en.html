🎵 AudioPlay - Pre-roll Metronome Guide

🎯 What is the pre-roll metronome?
The pre-roll metronome is a rhythmic countdown that plays before each song. It helps you prepare for the music tempo, ideal for musicians, dancers, or simply to get into the rhythm before the song starts!

🚀 Quick Setup

How to enable the metronome?

  1. Open Settings (⚙️ button in the interface)
  2. Find the "Playback" section
  3. Check the "Metronome before playback" checkbox
  4. Set the number of beats (1 to 16, recommended: 4)
  5. Click "Save"
  6. ✅ The metronome is now active!

⚙️ Beat Count Configuration

What is a "beat"?

A beat is one metronome click. For example:

  • 4 beats = 4 clicks before the song (most common)
  • 8 beats = 8 clicks (for more preparation time)
  • 1 beat = One quick click

Configuration Examples

Number of Beats Recommended Use Example
1-2 beats Quick start, slow music Ballad, ambient music
4 beats ⭐ Standard, 4/4 music Pop, rock, most songs
6-8 beats Complex music, waltz (3/4) Jazz, classical music
16 beats Long preparation, instrumental practice Musical exercises, rehearsals

🎼 Automatic BPM Synchronization

🎵 Smart Synchronization
The metronome automatically adapts to the BPM (Beats Per Minute) of each song. This means a fast song will have a fast metronome, and a slow song will have a slow metronome!

How does the metronome know the BPM?

The BPM must be calculated and saved beforehand:

  1. Open AudioPlay and load your audio files
  2. Select one or more songs in the list
  3. Right-click → "Calculate BPM"
  4. ⏳ AudioPlay analyzes the song (may take a few seconds)
  5. ✅ The BPM is detected and saved in metadata
  6. 🎵 The metronome will now use this BPM!
⚠️ Important: If a song doesn't have a calculated BPM, the metronome will use 120 BPM by default (average tempo). For the best experience, calculate the BPM for all your songs!

🎯 Detailed Operation

Playback Sequence with Metronome

  1. You click "Play" or double-click a song
  2. The metronome starts
    • First beat: higher pitch (🔊 1000 Hz) to mark the start
    • Following beats: standard pitch (🔊 800 Hz)
    • Tempo synchronized to the song's BPM
  3. Automatic silence removal
    • AudioPlay detects silence at the beginning of the song
    • Silence is automatically removed
  4. Music starts immediately after the last beat

Technical Sound Details

Feature Value
First beat (start) 1000 Hz (high pitch)
Following beats 800 Hz (standard pitch)
Each click duration 50 milliseconds
Volume 30% of maximum amplitude
Default BPM 120 BPM (if not calculated)

🎼 BPM Calculation

📊 Why calculate BPM?
BPM calculation allows the metronome to adapt perfectly to each song's tempo. Without calculated BPM, the metronome uses 120 BPM (average tempo) which may not match your music.

How to calculate BPM?

Method 1: Manual calculation for one song
  1. Select a song in the list
  2. Right-click on the song
  3. Choose "Calculate BPM"
  4. ⏳ Wait during analysis (a few seconds)
  5. ✅ BPM appears in the "BPM" column
Method 2: Automatic calculation
  1. In Settings > Playback
  2. Select the BPM calculation method:
    • Manual - You decide when to calculate
    • First playback - Calculates automatically on first play
    • Background - Progressively calculates all songs
  3. Click "Save"

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

The metronome is too loud or too quiet

Solution: The metronome volume is fixed at 30% of maximum amplitude. To adjust it, change your system's general volume before the music starts.

The metronome doesn't adapt to my song's tempo

Solution: Make sure the BPM has been calculated for this song. Right-click on the song → "Calculate BPM". Without calculated BPM, the metronome uses 120 BPM by default.

I don't hear the metronome

Check:

  • ✅ The "Metronome before playback" checkbox is checked in settings
  • ✅ The number of beats is greater than 0
  • ✅ Your system volume is sufficient
  • ✅ You clicked "Save" after modifying settings

Can I temporarily disable the metronome?

Yes! Simply uncheck the "Metronome before playback" checkbox in settings and click "Save". The metronome will be disabled until you enable it again.

🎯 Usage Tips

  • Start with 4 beats: It's the most common and natural configuration
  • Calculate your BPMs: Take time to calculate BPM for your music library
  • Adapt to usage: More beats for practice, fewer for casual listening
  • Listen for the first beat: The high pitch of the first beat signals the start
  • Use automatic method: Let AudioPlay calculate BPMs in the background
