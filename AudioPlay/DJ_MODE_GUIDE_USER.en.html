🎧 AudioPlay - DJ Mode Guide

Professional DJ Mode
Transform AudioPlay into a complete DJ mixing station with 2 decks, crossfader, effects and recording!

⚙️ Activating DJ Mode

  1. Open Settings in AudioPlay
  2. Check "DJ Mixer Mode"
  3. Click Save
  4. Restart AudioPlay
  5. The DJ interface opens automatically

🎛️ Main Features

1. Deck Control

Deck A and Deck B - Two independent decks with:

  • Load Track, Play/Pause/Stop, Cue
  • Volume and Position sliders
  • Interactive TrackBar for seeking and scratching

2. Crossfader & Mixing

  • Three curve modes: Linear, DJ, Sharp
  • VU-Meters: LED-style display with peak-hold

3. Pitch & Tempo

  • Pitch Bend: ±8% real-time adjustment
  • BPM Detection: Automatic tempo analysis
  • SYNC Button: Auto-sync between decks

4. Waveform Visualization

  • Real-time audio amplitude display
  • Cue markers (red dots) and position indicator (yellow line)
  • Click on waveform to jump to position

5. HotCues (8 per deck)

ActionShortcut
TriggerLeft Click
SetShift + Click
DeleteRight Click

Colors: Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Cyan, Blue, Magenta, Pink

6. Loop

  • Auto loops: 2, 4, 8, 16 beats (BPM-based)
  • Manual loops: Loop In, Loop Out, Loop On/Off

7. Mix Recording

  • WAV format: High-fidelity audio quality
  • Saved to: Documents\AudioPlay\Recordings\
  • Filename: Mix_YYYYMMDD_HHmmss.wav

8. Auto-Cue

  • Automatically detects first audio signal
  • Skips silence at track start
  • Adaptive RMS threshold

9. Sampler (8 Pads)

  • Load: Right-click on pad → Choose audio file
  • Trigger: Left-click to play
  • Multiple pads can play simultaneously

10. Audio Effects

Per-deck effects: Phaser, Reverb, Echo

Check/uncheck boxes to enable/disable. Effects can be combined!

💡 Pro Tips

  • Beat Matching: Use waveform to visually align beats
  • Strategic HotCues: Place cues at key points (intro, drop, outro)
  • Creative Loops: Use short loops to build transitions
  • VU-Meters: Keep levels in green/yellow zone (avoid red)
