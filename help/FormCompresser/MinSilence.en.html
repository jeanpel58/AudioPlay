🔉Minimal sustained silence (seconds)

This parameter sets the minimum continuous silence length the analyzer requires to consider a region as a valid cut point.

Why it exists

Short gaps or quiet passages should not be considered a track boundary. Requiring a minimal sustained silence avoids false positives caused by brief dips in volume.

Effect of changing it

  • Smaller value: more sensitive, may cut inside very short quiet parts.
  • Larger value: requires longer silence to accept a cut, safer but may miss short gaps.
Recommendation: default: 0.5s. Do not set below 0.05s to avoid instability.
